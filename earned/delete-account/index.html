You can delete your Earned. account and all your game data at any time. Deleting is permanent: your progress can't be brought back.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Earned. and go to the Life tab, then Stats.
  2. Scroll down to Account and tap Delete my game and account.
  3. Tap Delete for good to confirm.

Your account and data are removed from our server straight away.

By email

If you can't open the app anymore, email privacy@developrs.be with the subject "Delete my Earned. account" and your Player ID (shown in the app under Life → Stats → Account). Your account is anonymous, so the Player ID is the only way we can find it. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous player account and sign-in.
  • All game progress: money and its history, skills and XP, jobs, purchases, businesses, goals and stats.
  • The record of rewarded ads you watched.

What may be kept

  • Database backups, which are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Short-lived server logs (IP address, time of request), kept a few days by our hosting provider for security.
  • Crash reports, which don't contain your progress and are removed after at most 90 days.

Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your data on our server.
