Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Earned. is a wealth simulation game. You can't earn or spend real money in the game. We built it to need as little about you as possible: no name, no email address, no contacts, no location.

Who we are

Earned. is made by Rutger Strijbos, Schaffelberg, Heist-op-den-Berg, Belgium. We are the controller of the personal data described here under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Questions about privacy: info@developrs.be.

What we collect

1. A random player account

When you first open the game, our server creates an anonymous account for you. It is a random ID (for example 3f2a…) and a sign-in token. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple/Google account. The sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage so you stay signed in.

2. Your game progress

The game runs on our server, so it stores what you do in the game: your in-game money and its history, skills and XP, jobs, purchases, businesses, goals, settings that change the game, and the times at which you did things. We need the times to work out what you earned while you were away and to keep the game fair.

3. Technical data

Like any online service, our servers receive your IP address and basic request details (time, app version, device type) when the app talks to them. Our hosting provider keeps these in short-lived server logs to run and secure the service. We don't use them to identify you or build a profile.

4. Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads (for example a coffee break for extra Focus). Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show ads and count them, AdMob receives your device's advertising ID (if your phone allows it), IP address and basic device information. In the EU you are asked for consent before ads are personalised; you can say no and still watch ads, which are then non-personalised. On iOS we only use your advertising ID if you allow tracking in the system prompt. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings at any time. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Our server only records that you watched a rewarded ad and when, so it can give the reward and apply the daily limit.

5. Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report: what went wrong in the code, the app version, device model, operating system version and your random player ID. Crash reports don't contain your name or contacts. We use them only to fix bugs.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps a few things only on your phone: your sign-in token, whether sound is on, and which payslips, events and job openings you've already seen. We don't receive these.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, microphone or camera. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for profiling.

Who processes it for us

We use a few service providers. They process data on our behalf and under contract, and only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.): database, sign-in and server functions. Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.): rewarded ads. Privacy policy
  • Sentry (Functional Software Inc.): crash reports. Privacy policy
  • Apple and Google: app distribution through the App Store and Google Play, under their own privacy policies.

We only share data with others if the law requires it.

Where it's stored

Our game database is hosted in the European Union (Paris, France). Some providers above are based in the United States and may process data there. Where that happens, transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Player account and game progress: as long as you have an account. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Server logs: a few days, as set by our hosting provider.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Backups: our database backups are overwritten within 30 days, so deleted data disappears from them too.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all game data yourself, at any time, in the app:

  1. Open the Life tab and go to Stats.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my game and account.
  3. Confirm with Delete for good.

This removes your account and progress from our server straight away. It can't be undone.

Can't open the app anymore? See Delete your Earned. account to ask us by email.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your data on our server; it only removes the sign-in token from your phone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect what happened before). To use these rights, email us with your Player ID (in the app under Life → Stats → Account). Because your account is anonymous, the Player ID is how we know which data is yours. We answer within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be, or with the authority where you live.

Children

Earned. is not meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 uses the game, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If the game starts collecting something new (for example when you can link an Apple or Google account), we'll update this page and the date at the top first. For important changes we'll also tell you in the app.
